# W3C Strategic Highlights — September 2019

**World Wide Web Consortium (W3C)**  
*Advancing the Open Web Platform through collaboration, standards, and innovation.*

---

## 📘 Overview

The September 2019 edition of the **W3C Strategic Highlights** outlines key activities, priorities, and strategic directions shaping the **Web ecosystem**.  
This report provides insights into ongoing standards development, community engagement, and future initiatives to ensure a secure, accessible, and interoperable Web for all.

---

## 🎯 Mission

To lead the Web to its full potential by developing open standards that enable long-term growth, trust, and innovation across industries, devices, and communities worldwide.

---

## 🧩 Strategic Focus Areas

### 1. Core Web Technologies
- Continued advancement of **HTML**, **CSS**, and **JavaScript** specifications.  
- Ongoing improvements for interoperability, performance, and browser consistency.  
- Strengthening of testing and validation frameworks.

### 2. Web for All
- Enhancing **Web accessibility (WAI)** for users with disabilities.  
- Commitment to inclusive design and multilingual support.  
- Collaboration with public and private sectors to improve digital inclusion.

### 3. Security, Privacy & Trust
- Development of stronger **Web Authentication** and **Privacy** APIs.  
- Initiatives to mitigate tracking and fingerprinting.  
- Ongoing efforts to standardize secure communications (TLS, HTTPS).

### 4. Media & Real-Time Communications
- Continued evolution of **WebRTC**, **Media Source Extensions**, and **Encrypted Media Extensions**.  
- Support for low-latency streaming and adaptive bitrate content delivery.

### 5. Web of Things (WoT)
- Enabling interoperability across IoT devices and services.  
- Advancing **WoT Thing Description**, **Scripting API**, and related specifications.

### 6. Data & Semantics
- Strengthening **Linked Data** and **Semantic Web** capabilities.  
- Improving interoperability for data exchange and metadata integration.  
- Collaboration on **JSON-LD**, **RDF**, and related vocabularies.

### 7. Publishing & Digital Content
- Evolution of **EPUB** and **Web Publications** standards.  
- Integration with core Web technologies to ensure accessibility and longevity of digital books.

### 8. Web Performance & User Experience
- Ongoing work on **Web Performance APIs** and **Progressive Web Apps (PWAs)**.  
- Enhancements to responsiveness, speed, and offline functionality.

---

## 🧠 Emerging Areas

- **Machine Learning on the Web** – enabling in-browser AI and model execution.  
- **Immersive Web (AR/VR)** – expanding the **WebXR** ecosystem for new user experiences.  
- **Payments & Fintech** – secure, interoperable digital payment standards.  
- **Automotive Web Platform** – integrating vehicle data and services using Web technologies.

---

## 👥 Community & Participation

W3C fosters global participation through:
- **Working Groups** – formal specification development.  
- **Community & Business Groups** – incubating new ideas and technologies.  
- **Advisory Committees** – providing strategic and policy guidance.

Participation is open to industry, academia, government, and individual experts.
